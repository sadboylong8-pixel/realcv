2. **Local Development Server** (optional):

## 🌐 How to Publish Online for FREE (To Share Link with Employers)

Having a live link like `honglylong.github.io` on your CV gives immense credit during interviews. Here are two easy 2-minute options:

### Option 1: GitHub Pages (Recommended)
1. Create a free account on [GitHub.com](https://github.com) if you haven't already.
2. Create a new repository named `honglylong-portfolio` (or `<your-username>.github.io`).
3. Upload `index.html` and `README.md`.
4. Go to **Settings > Pages**, choose `main` branch, and click **Save**.
5. Your portfolio is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/honglylong-portfolio`!

### Option 2: Netlify Drop (Instant Drag & Drop)
1. Go to [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop).
2. Drag and drop this folder (`d:\REAL CV\CV`).
3. Netlify will instantly give you a free live URL (e.g. `https://hongly-it-support.netlify.app`) that you can put right under your name on your CV!
